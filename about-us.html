Turning raw marks into insight

Why we built NEET Ranker

Most NEET aspirants take dozens of mock tests over their preparation, but by the tenth or twentieth test, the scores start to blur together in a notebook or a messy spreadsheet. It's easy to see that a score went up or down — much harder to see why. NEET Ranker exists to close that gap: log a test in a couple of minutes, tag why each question was missed, and let the dashboard find the pattern you can't see from inside a single test.

Part of the NEELAXMI project

NEET Ranker is developed under NEELAXMI THE UNION, an ecosystem of free tools built for students. The project's guiding idea — reflected in the logo you see across the site — is a simple one: raw effort, organized well, is what actually moves a rank.

Independent and unofficial

NEET Ranker is an independent, community-built tool. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or connected to the National Testing Agency (NTA) or any official body that conducts or administers NEET. Every rank and percentile figure shown is a self-calculated estimate for personal study use — always refer to official NTA communications for anything that matters for your actual exam or admission.

Open source

The NEET Ranker codebase is released under the MIT License, in keeping with NEELAXMI's practice of building tools in the open.
